# CurioHub brand direction

Positioning: A joyful neighbourhood hub where children explore interests and build skills, while parents find structure, care, and convenience.

Core line: A little curious. A lot possible.
Supporting line: Where curiosity comes alive.

## Visual system
- Deep teal #183C44: structure, navigation, trust, primary text.
- Warm coral #F36C55: discovery, expressive headlines, primary discovery actions.
- Sunshine #F9D36F: optimism, accents, celebratory moments.
- Cream #FFFAF3: calm backgrounds and comfortable reading.
- Sage #ECEFDD: play and gentle supporting sections.
- Manrope for headings; DM Sans for body and controls; occasional italic Georgia for warmth.
- Rounded photography, hand-drawn flourishes, generous spacing, and clear navigation.
- Supplied rocket/book/lightbulb logo remains the brand mark. The text wordmark complements it.

## Voice
Warm, clear, curious, and reassuring. Speak to parents while celebrating the child. Use concrete activity descriptions and visible age recommendations. Avoid performance pressure, invented outcomes, testimonials, or safety certifications.

## Product and launch
Three experiences: structured learning, workshops/events, and supervised exploration.
Age groups: 3–6, 6–10, and 10–15.
Hyderabad school-partner pilot first; flagship and franchise centres are planned future phases.
Pricing, curriculum, weekly timetable, membership benefits, and quarterly discount are illustrative proposals for review.

## Preview behavior
Browser-local basket supports multiple sessions, removal, a combined indicative total, request preparation, and enquiry downloads. Membership preference is stored locally. No payment, account creation, live availability, confirmed booking, outbound email, or subscriber registration is connected.
Parent identifying details are not persisted by the booking form. Interest forms only create a local download. Live operation will require approved venue and dates, booking inventory, a backend, payment integration, notifications, and final customer policies.
